(Suzanne & James Robertson), 5 Whys (Taiichi Ohno), and the 7-label review taxonomy
(adapted from carollrollroll/prd-skill). Per-source notes, what was modified, and the
safety review log live in `skills/req-dev-lite/references/attribution.md` (Chinese).
No source text is reproduced; concepts and question shapes were re-expressed and extended.
